Healthcare

Egypt · 9 campaigns · USD

Al Amal Medical Center

Nine service-led campaigns that turned a multi-service clinic into a steady stream of patient conversations.

532Patient conversations
$2.28Avg cost per conversation
$0.81Best campaign cost

Challenge

A multi-service clinic competing for local patients against larger hospital brands. Dentistry, wellness, maternity and home care each needed a different audience and a different message — one clinic-wide campaign was never going to serve all of them.

Strategy

Split the account by service line so every service had its own creative, audience and budget. Lead with conversation objectives rather than link clicks, so enquiries land in the inbox where the front desk can convert them the same day.

Solution

Nine campaigns across wellness packages, braces, pregnancy care, home service and seasonal Ramadan and National Day offers. Reels carried reach, message ads carried conversion, and every line of copy was written to clear Meta's health-category policy on first review.

Real estate

Egypt & Oman · 3 campaigns · USD

Al-Abrar Real Estate

Property lead generation across two markets for a developer with twenty years in Oman.

277Qualified leads
$7.84Avg cost per lead
$5.66Best campaign CPL

Challenge

A long-established Omani developer expanding its reach into the Egyptian market. Two countries, two very different costs per lead, and a brand that needed to feel established in one market while being introduced in the other.

Strategy

Separate campaign structures per market rather than one blended account, so budgets could not cannibalise each other. Lead forms for enquiry volume; engagement campaigns around national moments to build brand equity cheaply.

Solution

Three campaigns: an Egypt-market lead generation push, a dedicated 400 Villa project campaign running instant forms, and an Oman National Day engagement campaign that earned 28,653 post engagements for under a hundred dollars.

Real estate investment

Egypt · 6 campaigns · EGP

Invest House

Restructuring the account cut cost per enquiry by 63% — without changing the creative.

208Messaging conversations
EGP 18.48Best cost per conversation
63%Reduction vs. baseline

Challenge

An investment-focused developer whose ads read like every other listing in the feed. Cost per enquiry had settled above EGP 50, which made the channel hard to justify at scale.

Strategy

Reposition the messaging around the investment promise rather than the unit specification. Then split campaigns by project and unit type, move conversations into Messenger and WhatsApp where intent is higher, and test account structure — not just creative.

Solution

Six campaigns spanning villas, apartments and multi-unit projects, including a call-ads campaign for buyers who prefer the phone. The rebuilt villa campaign landed conversations at EGP 18.48 against a baseline above EGP 50.

Education

Egypt · 4 campaigns · EGP

Nerds Academy

Changing the conversion channel — not the creative — nearly halved cost per enquiry.

58Enrolment conversations
EGP 135.68Best cost per conversation
48%Reduction vs. previous

Challenge

Enrolment demand concentrated around intake dates, with cost per enquiry sitting above EGP 250. At the academy's price point that maths does not work for long.

Strategy

Test the conversion channel rather than iterating on creative alone. Move enquiries out of in-app messaging and into WhatsApp, where parents and adult learners already talk, and keep the Instagram profile warm between intakes with a low-cost traffic campaign.

Solution

Four campaigns across two intakes. The WhatsApp campaign brought cost per conversation down to EGP 135.68 from EGP 261.25, and became the default structure for the account. A parallel traffic campaign delivered 184 Instagram profile visits at EGP 3.78 each.
